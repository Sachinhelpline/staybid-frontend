## PHASE 3: DETAILED DISCUSSION (Week 2)

### Call Script (Ayushi + Investor, 20-30 min)

**Opening (2 min):**
> Thanks for your interest! I'm Ayushi, leading investor relations for our Circle launch. Today I want to make sure this model fits your investment goals. Let me walk you through how it works and answer any questions. Cool?

**Section 1: The Model (5 min)**

> Here's the simple version: You invest ₹3 lakhs with us. That money goes toward securing a premium room in one of our three managed properties — Dhanaulti, Rishikesh, or Mussoorie. Each property operates year-round but has a different peak season. So when Dhanaulti is in off-season, Rishikesh is heating up. That means you're always earning.
>
> Guests book through StayBid. We handle all the operations — checkins, checkouts, cleaning, guest support. You just own the room and earn your share of the booking revenue. Simple.

**Section 2: The Properties (5 min)**

> **Dhanaulti:** Premium hill resort, 10 cottages, peaks Mar-Jun (spring/summer holidays). Average ₹45k/night.
>
> **Rishikesh:** Wellness retreat, 10 rooms, peaks Oct-Nov (autumn travel season). Average ₹30k/night.
>
> **Mussoorie:** Adventure lodge, 10 rooms, peaks Jun-Sep (summer escape season). Average ₹35k/night.
>
> Together, they cover all 12 months. When one is slow, the other two are busy. That's the "Always in Season" philosophy.

**Section 3: The Money (7 min)**

> Your ₹3 lakh investment covers one room for 12 months. Let's break down the money:
>
> **Monthly cost to us:** ₹30-45k (depending on room category)
> **Expected monthly payout to you:** ₹30-40k (highly depends on occupancy)
> **Platform fee:** 12% of guest bookings (this goes to StayBid ops)
>
> So if your room generates ₹50k in bookings in a month, you get ₹50k − (₹50k × 12%) = ₹44k. Minus the ₹45k room cost, you're close to break-even on that month but ahead by year's end because occupancy and rates fluctuate.
>
> **Important:** These numbers are ILLUSTRATIVE and based on actual bookings. If occupancy is low, payouts are lower. We don't guarantee returns. What we guarantee is transparency and a fully managed property.

**Section 4: Your Returns (3 min)**

> Over 12 months, the expected annual payout is **₹30-40k/month × 12 = ₹3.6-4.8L**. Minus your ₹3L investment, you're looking at **₹60-180k in net gains over the year**, depending on bookings. That's roughly **10-15% annual return**. Again, illustrative — depends on actual guest demand.

**Section 5: What Happens Next (2 min)**

> If you're interested, here's the timeline:
>
> 1. **Week 1 (Aug 20-24):** KYC documents (ID, address, bank, PAN)
> 2. **Week 2 (Aug 25-31):** Legal agreement signed (3 copies, keep one)
> 3. **Week 3 (Sep 1-7):** Razorpay payment, funds secured
> 4. **Week 4+ (Sep 8+):** Properties go live, your room starts taking bookings
> 5. **Month 1+ (Sep onward):** Monthly payout arrives in your bank (settle first checkout, then monthly recurring)
>
> Total onboarding: 3 weeks.

**Closing (1 min):**
> Do you have any questions before I send over the KYC forms?

## FAQS FOR INVESTORS

**Q: Is my money safe?**
A: Your ₹3L is held in a StayBid escrow account. It's legally secured under the investment agreement and covers your room's annual cost. At year's end, any remaining balance (or owed amounts) are settled.

**Q: Can I withdraw before 12 months?**
A: No, there's a 1-year lock. This ensures properties run smoothly without sudden room shortages. After 12 months, you can withdraw or renew.

**Q: How are payouts calculated?**
A: Monthly payouts = (guest bookings on your room − 12% platform fee − your room cost). If guests generate ₹50k, you get ₹50k − (₹50k × 12%) − ₹30k = ₹8.8k that month.

**Q: What if my room doesn't get bookings?**
A: We manage demand 24/7 and optimize pricing. Plus, 3-property diversification (different peak seasons) smooths occupancy. If occupancy is low, payouts are lower — which is why we emphasize "illustrative, not guaranteed" returns.

**Q: Can I sell my room/investment?**
A: Not during the 1-year lock. After 12 months, you can exit or renew. Resale to other investors is a future feature.

**Q: What if I want to add more rooms?**
A: Great! After successful completion of Year 1, you can invest in additional properties or rooms in a second round.
