### PAGE 24 — Step 3: Rooms & Pricing
**IMAGE PROMPT:**
> A rooms-builder page, A4 landscape 4K, cream. Center: a stack of 2 **room-type cards** being built — each with a category chip (Deluxe / Suite / Family), room name, max guests, quantity, a **base price** field and a **floor price** field (the lowest a bid can win at), plus in-room amenity chips (AC, WiFi, breakfast). A small **AI pricing** badge glows: *"AI suggests a live price daily."* Gold "+ Add room type" button.

**PAGE BODY TEXT:** For each room category you set the guest capacity, how many units you have, a **base (rack) price**, and a **floor price** — the lowest a reverse-auction bid is allowed to win at. You never show the floor to guests; it just protects your margin. StayBid's **AI pricing** then suggests a smart daily "live price" based on demand, season and competitor rates — always kept below the big travel sites so you win the booking.
