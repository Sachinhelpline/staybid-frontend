### PAGE 32 — Auto-Provisioning → Operated Hotel
**IMAGE PROMPT:**
> A transformation infographic, A4 landscape 4K, cream + gold. A left-to-right flow: a **paper listing** card → a gold gear/magic burst labelled "Provision" → a **live operated hotel** card (with rooms, physical room-units grid, a scorecard medal marked "Draft"). Small chips along the flow: "Creates hotel", "Builds rooms", "Auto-creates room units", "Owner gets dashboard access", "Goes live when ops flips it on". Clean and satisfying.

**PAGE BODY TEXT:** Approving a lease-out **provisions** a genuine hotel behind the scenes: StayBid creates the hotel record, builds its room categories, and auto-generates the physical **room units** (so inventory is trackable to the unit). The original owner is granted **dashboard access** to watch performance. The hotel starts as a **draft** — fully manageable but hidden from guests — until operations flips it live, so nothing half-ready ever reaches a traveller.
