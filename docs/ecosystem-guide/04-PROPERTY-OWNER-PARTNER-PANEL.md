**PAGE BODY TEXT:** Rooms & Pricing is where AI does the heavy lifting. For each room type you keep a base and a protected floor price; StayBid computes a smart **live price** daily from demand, season, occupancy and — importantly — the cheapest competitor price it scrapes, so StayBid's rate is always the lowest visible. You can always override, but most owners let the AI run.
